Yifei Sun

MSCS, Stanford University

I am Yifei Sun, an MSCS student at Stanford University working on AI for science and AI for healthcare.

At Stanford, I work with Prof. Emily Alsentzer on AI for healthcare, focusing on EHR benchmarking and foundation model training; and with Prof. Jure Leskovec on generative models for protein design.

I completed my undergraduate studies in Computer Science and Mathematics at the University of Toronto. I conducted research under Prof. Rahul G. Krishnan on healthcare AI; with Prof. Sheila McIlraith on reinforcement learning; and Prof. Ashton Anderson on knowledge graphs.

Previously, I spent a year at Cerebras Systems working on ML compilers as a machine learning stack engineer. Before that, I interned at Cannacord Genuity.

Research

Research Interests

AI for Science

Building generative models for protein design, coupling frozen pretrained models through lightweight, parameter-efficient adapters so that large models can work together without retraining.

Clinical Foundation Models

Training foundation models on electronic health records that generalize across hospitals and support robust, privacy-preserving deployment.

EHR Benchmarking & Evaluation

Designing better benchmarks and ways to evaluate AI on real-world EHR data, so that progress in clinical AI is measured faithfully.

Bio

About Me

I made several pivots in my academic and professional journey.

Finance: I began in financial mathematics (MAEF at UofT), but my internship, even though it was a great experience, showed me I could pursue a different path.

SWE: I spent a rewarding year at Cerebras Systems working on compilers, yet the rapid rise of LLMs made me reconsider whether a traditional software engineering track was the future I wanted.

Research: I moved into research, and while the process has been challenging, it has been deeply inspiring to develop new methods and earn acceptance at top-tier venues. I am driven to contribute work that can make a real impact.
